COMMON QUESTIONS

We're here to help.

Find quick answers to some of the questions we hear most often. Still have questions? Our team is just a call or chat away.

  • When can I enroll in Medicare?

    Most people first become eligible for Medicare at age 65. Your Initial Enrollment Period is the 7-month window that starts three months before the month you turn 65, includes your birthday month, and ends three months after. You can also enroll during the Annual Enrollment Period (October 15 – December 7) or a Special Enrollment Period if you qualify.

  • How do I choose the right health insurance plan?

    Start with your needs: the doctors and hospitals you use, the prescriptions you take, and the budget you're comfortable with. Compare plans on coverage type, network, premiums, deductibles, and out-of-pocket maximums. Our advisors can walk you through the options side by side so you can choose with confidence.

  • Do I really need life insurance?

    If people depend on your income — a spouse, children, aging parents, or even a mortgage — life insurance protects them if something happens to you. The amount you need depends on your responsibilities, debts, and goals. A short conversation with an advisor can show you exactly what coverage makes sense.

  • Is there a cost to work with your advisors?

    No. Our guidance is always at no cost to you. We're compensated by insurance carriers, and our job is to find the plan that fits your needs and budget — not to sell you more coverage than you need.

  • How can I speak with a licensed advisor?

    Call us at 1 (800) 123-4567, start a chat, or fill out our quick form and we'll reach out. A licensed advisor will get back to you — usually within one business day.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick Answers for Your Medicare Questions.

  • When can I enroll in Medicare?

    Most people first become eligible at 65. Your Initial Enrollment Period runs from three months before your 65th birthday through three months after it.

  • What's the difference between Medicare Advantage and Medigap?

    Medicare Advantage (Part C) replaces Original Medicare with an all-in-one plan that often adds drug and extra benefits. Medigap (Medicare Supplement) works alongside Original Medicare to help cover gaps like copays, coinsurance, and deductibles.

  • Do I need a separate plan for prescription drugs?

    Original Medicare (Parts A and B) doesn't cover most prescription drugs. You'll want either a Part D plan or a Medicare Advantage plan that includes drug coverage.

  • Is there a cost to work with your advisors?

    No — our advice is free, and there's never a cost to you for working with a licensed advisor.

  • Can you help me compare plans in my area?

    Absolutely. We'll look at the plans available in your zip code, compare them against your needs, and help you pick the best fit.
