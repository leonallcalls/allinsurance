FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Quick Answers for Your Medicare Questions.

  • When can I enroll in Medicare?

    Most people first become eligible at 65. Your Initial Enrollment Period runs from three months before your 65th birthday through three months after it.

  • What's the difference between Medicare Advantage and Medigap?

    Medicare Advantage (Part C) replaces Original Medicare with an all-in-one plan that often adds drug and extra benefits. Medigap (Medicare Supplement) works alongside Original Medicare to help cover gaps like copays, coinsurance, and deductibles.

  • Do I need a separate plan for prescription drugs?

    Original Medicare (Parts A and B) doesn't cover most prescription drugs. You'll want either a Part D plan or a Medicare Advantage plan that includes drug coverage.

  • Is there a cost to work with your advisors?

    No — our advice is free, and there's never a cost to you for working with a licensed advisor.

  • Can you help me compare plans in my area?

    Absolutely. We'll look at the plans available in your zip code, compare them against your needs, and help you pick the best fit.
